Behind The Brand

Hi, I'm Suhail.

Founder & Creative Director

My creative journey started when I was 14, learning graphic design and exploring how ideas could be turned into something visual. What began as curiosity slowly became freelance work, real projects and eventually a much bigger creative journey.

Over time, I discovered photography and filmmaking. The camera gave me a different way to tell stories — not by creating every moment, but by noticing the real ones already happening.

Where It Started

Lenso.

Before Lensography, there was Lenso — the beginning of my creative journey.

Lenso started as my freelance creative identity when I was 14. It became the place where I learned through real work, experimented with design and understood what it meant to build something of my own.

As my interests grew from design into photography and filmmaking, that same creative foundation evolved into Lensography — a space dedicated to documenting people, emotions and stories.
