Founder & Lead Photographer — Lensography

I'm a photographer and creative who believes the best photographs aren't simply the ones that look beautiful — they're the ones that make you feel something.

Starting young taught me to learn by doing, adapt quickly and notice the small details. Today, those lessons influence the way I photograph every celebration and connect with the people in front of my camera.

For me, photography isn't about controlling every second of a wedding. It's about knowing when to guide, when to observe and when to simply let a real moment happen.

Our Story

The story
behind
Lensography.

Lensography grew from a simple love for creating into a purpose — preserving stories that people can return to long after the celebration is over.

Before photography became the focus, the journey began in the world of design and visual creativity. That early experience shaped the way we understand composition, details and storytelling today.

As photography and filmmaking became a bigger part of the journey, the focus shifted from creating visuals to documenting something more meaningful — people, relationships and real emotions.

That evolution became Lensography: a wedding photography and film brand built around honest storytelling, personal experiences and memories that remain meaningful beyond the wedding day.
